How to play

You are the President. A conflict is under way that you would prefer nobody called a war. Each scene gives you three choices, and every choice moves five stats at once. Survive to the end of the run and you win.

Frequently asked questions

What do the five stats mean?

Approval — your rating. Drop to 30% and you are finished. The war drags it down every scene, and the drag gets worse the longer the conflict runs.

Dow — the markets. Crash them and the game ends.

Grift — money extracted. This is your score, not a safety net.

PM Yahooo — his mood. Anger him while The Files meter is high and the documents drop.

Congress — their attention. Reach zero and the War Powers Resolution passes.

What are the two meters below the stats?

📂 The Files — how close PM Yahooo is to releasing what he is holding. At 8 it triggers a crisis scene.

🌡️ Heat — investigative pressure. Reach 10 and the Select Committee has enough. Taking money raises it; it cools on its own over time.

I keep losing. What am I doing wrong?

Most likely you are taking every payday. Pure greed loses essentially every time — the Heat meter catches up long before the end. The strongest approaches protect Congress or your approval rating and take money only when the heat is already low.

Why are the scenes different every time I play?

That is deliberate. The game holds 32 scenarios and each run draws a different subset of about 24, so no two playthroughs are the same. A handful of key scenes always appear so the story still holds together.

Does the game save my progress?

No. A run takes about 15 minutes and is meant to be played in one sitting. Nothing is written to your device or to any server.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app makes no network requests at all and works identically in Airplane Mode.

Are these real people?

No. Every character is a fictional composite and every country in the game is invented. The game is satire set in an alternate timeline, not a depiction of real events or real individuals.

Is there anything to buy inside the app?

No. There are no in-app purchases, no advertising, no subscriptions and no accounts. You buy it once and that is the whole transaction.

Accessibility

The game uses the system font at standard sizes with high-contrast gold and cream text on dark backgrounds. If you have difficulty reading any part of the interface, please email us — we read accessibility reports first.
