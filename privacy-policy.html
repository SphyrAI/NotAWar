Privacy Policy

Not A War™  ·  Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version: Not A War collects nothing, stores nothing about you, sends nothing anywhere, and has no internet connection. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

1. Who we are

Not A War™ ("the app") is published by Robert John Green ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the address in the Contact section below.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, store or process any personal data whatsoever. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses or contact details
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or IP addresses
  • Location data of any kind
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
  • Photos, contacts, calendars, microphone or camera data
  • Purchase history or financial information

3. Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, notifications or any other protected resource, because it does not use any of them.

4. Network access

The app makes no network requests. It contains no web views, no remote content, no servers and no third-party SDKs. It functions identically with your device in Airplane Mode, because it never needed a connection in the first place.

5. Third parties

There are none. The app embeds no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, no crash-reporting services and no social media kits. No data is shared with anyone, because no data exists to share.

6. Game progress

Your progress exists only in memory while the app is running and is discarded when you close it. Nothing is written to disk, to iCloud, or to any server.

7. Children's privacy

The app is rated 12+ for infrequent or mild profanity and mature satirical themes, and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting nothing.

8. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, delete or port. If you contact us to exercise these rights, our answer will be that we have no record of you, and that is by design rather than by omission.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
